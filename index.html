  • Ran Google Ads campaigns for partners in the UK and the Netherlands, and used funnel analysis and conversion rate optimization to improve results, tracking each assessment step so we could see where people dropped off.
  • Localized the partner campaign guide for different countries based on local sales insights.
  • Designed the platform's partner pitch deck and scripted a two-minute explainer video for internal stakeholders.

How I work

Understand the business, build the strategy, make the data work

1 · Understand the business

Every plan starts with what the business needs to win: pipeline in target accounts, a product launch, footfall in stores. I run brand positioning, ideal customer profile and persona workshops with clients and account teams before planning begins.

2 · Build and run the strategy

I turn that into a channel plan and run it end to end, across ABM, paid media, content, events and influencers. When speed matters, I make the creative in-house: static posts, infographics, ads in every size and video edits.

Adobe Creative Suite · Figma · Canva · PlayPlay

3 · Read the data and make it work

I track every step, compare each channel against a benchmark and change what isn't working mid-campaign. For a food-franchise client, analyzing ad exports in Python showed that a frequency of 2–3, video, and an 8 pm to midnight search slot performed best. Budgets moved to match.
