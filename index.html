The brief

About 80% of the company's business went through partners. Its new platform gave each partner a co-branded page with a three-minute, six-question assessment: a prospect answers, gets a product recommendation, and becomes a lead for that partner. Partners paid for the traffic from their own budgets, and our agency ran the campaigns for them. Fifteen partners in the UK, US, Netherlands, Germany and Ireland signed up to the program.

What I did

  • Wrote the Google search campaign playbook used across partners: search split into service, competitor and brand campaigns over five ad groups; separate desktop and mobile display campaigns; and a 20:80 search-to-display split by impressions.
  • Planned each partner's campaign: personas, budget, a click-to-lead forecast, and a LinkedIn test that ran multiple ads in month one and kept only the winner.
  • Ran Google Ads campaigns for partners in the UK and the Netherlands, and used funnel analysis and conversion rate optimization to improve results, tracking each assessment step so we could see where people dropped off.
  • Localized the partner campaign guide for different countries based on local sales insights.
  • Designed the platform's partner pitch deck and scripted a two-minute explainer video for internal stakeholders.
